Delete Your Data

For the Dog Runner: Temple Escape app by BivoGames (package com.bivogames.dogrunner) · Updated 13 November 2026

Dog Runner has no sign-up and no accounts, and BivoGames keeps no player data on its own servers. Your game progress is saved only on your phone. Scores and achievements you share through Google Play Games are kept by Google. Follow the steps below to delete either one, or email us and we will help.

1.Delete your game progress on your phone

  1. Open Dog Runner.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Reset ALL progress, then tap RESET to confirm.

You can also uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Dog Runner → Storage → Clear data. Either way, the progress is deleted from your phone right away.

2.Delete your Google Play Games data

If you used Google Play Games with Dog Runner, your scores and achievements for the game are stored by Google. To delete them:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app and go to your profile.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, choose Dog Runner and tap Delete.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can also manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

3.Ask us to help

Send an email to bibhusakal85649073@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Dog Runner". We will reply within 30 days and explain how to remove your data. We do not need your name, password or any other personal details to do this.
