BivoGames · Dog Runner: Temple Escape

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Covers the Android app (package com.bivogames.dogrunner) and the web version of the game.

In short

  • You don't need an account to play, and we don't collect your name, email or location.
  • Your progress (bones, dogs, upgrades, scores) is saved only on your own device.
  • The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.
  • If you use Google Play Games, Google handles your scores and achievements.
  • You can delete everything at any time from the game's Settings.

1.Who we are

Dog Runner: Temple Escape ("Dog Runner", "the game") is made by BivoGames ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, where it goes and the choices you have.

2.Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your phone or in your browser:

  • Bones and golden bones
  • Dogs you own and their levels
  • Dog food upgrades
  • Best score and distance
  • Your 10 best runs
  • Achievements
  • Daily tasks and Daily Treat streak
  • Sound and graphics settings

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to BivoGames, and we have no servers that store it. It is protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock. It stays saved for as long as the game is installed, even if you don't play for a long time.

3.Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no sign-up, chat, profile, camera, microphone, contacts or location features.

4.Google Play Games

The Android app supports Google Play Games Services for leaderboards and achievements. If you use Play Games, Google may sign you in automatically. When you finish a run, the game sends your score, distance and unlocked achievements to Google so that leaderboards and achievements can be shown.

Google processes your Play Games profile (for example your gamer name and avatar) under the Google Privacy Policy. BivoGames does not receive your email address or real name. You can change who sees your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

5.Other services the game uses

  • Google Play delivers and updates the app. If you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may collect crash reports and send us anonymous totals, such as how often the app crashes.
  • Google Fonts. When you are online, the game may load its fonts from Google's servers. As with any web request, these servers receive basic technical information such as your IP address and device or browser type.
  • jsDelivr. The web version loads its 3D engine (three.js) from the jsDelivr content network, which receives the same kind of technical request information. The Android app includes the engine and does not need it.

These providers handle that information under their own privacy policies.

6.Ads and purchases

The current version of Dog Runner shows no ads and contains no advertising software. Everything in the game is earned by playing, and there are no in-app purchases. If we add ads in a future version (for example Google AdMob), we will update this policy and the game's Google Play listing before that version is released. The update will explain what the ad provider collects and what choices you have.

7.Children

Dog Runner is a family-friendly game for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8.App permissions

The Android app only uses network access, to load fonts and to connect to Google Play Games. It does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

9.Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until you delete it. To remove everything, open the game and go to Settings → Reset ALL progress. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage also deletes it. To remove Play Games scores and achievements, use the Google Play Games settings or your Google account. Step-by-step instructions are on our Delete Your Data page.

10.Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because BivoGames does not hold personal data about players, there is normally nothing for us to send or delete. You can still email us (see Contact below) with any request or question, and we will reply.

11.Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before that version is released.
